# 🎓 Career-Lock India
> **Discover Your Path. Know Your Worth. Build Your Future.**

Career-Lock India is an interactive career discovery, placement intelligence, and financial ROI platform built specifically for Indian college students (Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3), fresh graduates, and career switchers.

---

## 🚩 The Problem
In the Indian higher education ecosystem:
1. **Misleading Placement Hype**: High advertised CTC packages (e.g., ₹45 LPA) often conceal a modest ₹60,000/month in-hand salary once 4-year ESOPs and joining bonuses are factored out.
2. **The Tier Divide**: Tier-3 students frequently lack transparent roadmaps detailing off-campus recruitment versus on-campus eligibility.
3. **High Preparation Debt**: Students enroll in expensive certifications without a clear understanding of the payback timeline.

---

## ⚡ Core Features

- **🎯 Interactive Career Discovery Engine**: Recommends practical pathways based on branch, graduation year, college tier, and monthly preparation budget.
- **🔍 14 In-Depth Career Tracks**: Comprehensive entry guides across Software Engineering, Data, Cloud/DevOps, AI/ML, QA Automation, and Product Analytics.
- **💰 CTC Reality Check Simulator**: Breaks down advertised annual CTC into fixed base pay, variable bonus, EPF deductions, gratuity, and estimated monthly in-hand deposit.
- **📊 Career ROI & Payback Calculator**: Compares total preparation and tuition costs against post-tax savings with interactive 5-year break-even cash-flow charts.
- **🗺️ Interactive Milestone Roadmap**: Live checklists tracking foundational, technical, and portfolio readiness scores.
- **📋 Diagnostic Scorecard Generator**: Lead diagnostic evaluation providing a personalized breakdown of skill gaps and target compensation.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 18 with Vite
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS & shadcn/ui design patterns
- **Data Visualizations**: Recharts
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Forms & Validation**: React Hook Form with Zod schema validation
- **Notifications**: Sonner
